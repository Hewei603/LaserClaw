# Code of Conduct

LaserClaw contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific, and evidence-based.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss code, designs, tests, and risks directly.
- Give reproducible examples when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy and do not share real lab data, credentials, or private documents.
- Be clear when a recommendation affects safety, access control, or data retention.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private keys, private datasets, uploaded documents, or user data.
- Encouraging unsafe laser operation or presenting AI output as a substitute for SOPs and qualified review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, pull requests, comments, or contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to loss of contribution privileges.
