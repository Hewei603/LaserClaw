# Synthetic Troubleshooting Case Notes

This synthetic document is for LaserClaw benchmark and resume metrics only.

## CASE-LOW-POWER

Low output power after realignment is often caused by dirty optics, pump coupling drift, thermal lensing, or an output coupler mismatch. The first low-risk checks are cleaning inspection, pump spot position, and cavity mirror angle rollback.

## CASE-BEAM-TAIL

A tailed or elliptical beam spot can indicate clipping, thermal lensing, or a damaged coating. Compare beam profile images before and after each mirror adjustment.

## CASE-STABILITY

Power stability reports should include sampling duration, mean power, standard deviation, peak-to-peak drift, and any excluded intervals. Stability data must be attached to the case record.
