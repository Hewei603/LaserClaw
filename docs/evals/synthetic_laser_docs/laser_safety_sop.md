# Synthetic Laser Safety SOP

This synthetic document is for LaserClaw benchmark and resume metrics only. It is not a real laboratory policy, safety manual, or training material.

## SAF-PPE-1064

Class 4 alignment at 1030-1100 nm requires laser eyewear with OD >= 5. The recommended synthetic eyewear identifier is IR-1064-OD5. The eyewear must be checked before enabling pump current, and mismatched OD is a stop condition.

## SAF-AUTH-L3

High-power Class 4 operation requires L3 authorization and a Watcher in the room. The Watcher must be able to reach the emergency stop and must not be the operator performing alignment.

## SAF-EMERGENCY-EYE

If suspected eye exposure occurs, the first action is to press the emergency stop. The operator must notify the lab safety owner and seek medical evaluation within 15 minutes.

## SAF-BEAM-DUMP

Beam dump integrity must be checked before daily startup. The checklist includes surface damage, clamp tightness, thermal marks, and correct placement at every open beam path termination.

## SAF-RED-TAG

Red-tag shutdown conditions include missing interlock, eyewear OD mismatch, unknown reflected beam, damaged beam dump, or unauthorized personnel inside the controlled area.
